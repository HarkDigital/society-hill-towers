Building footprints and streets © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Street trees: Philadelphia Parks & Recreation Tree Inventory 2025; streetlights: Streets Department Street Poles inventory (203,000 poles with real heights and bulb types); parkland: PPR Properties boundaries; historic districts and neighborhoods: City of Philadelphia via OpenDataPhilly (CC-BY); bike share status: Indego / Bicycle Transit Systems. Traffic volumes: PennDOT Roadway Management System annual average daily traffic (RMSTRAFFIC, open data); the cars are synthesized typical flows at those volumes, not live vehicle positions. Vehicle positions: SEPTA real-time API. Live flights: adsb.fi community ADS-B data. Live ships: AIS via aisstream.io. Weather: Open-Meteo. Address search: Nominatim / OpenStreetMap. This is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by SEPTA or Indego. Towers modeled procedurally from photographs and published dimensions. Rendered with three.js. This is an interpretive model, not a survey.
A living model of

Philadelphia

Every block of the city at its true measured height, a quarter-million buildings from the Delaware to the Schuylkill, with SEPTA's buses and trolleys moving through the streets live. Take the controls and fly it: the skyline, the rivers, the rowhouse blocks.
