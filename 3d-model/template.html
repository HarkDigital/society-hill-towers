The sky follows Philadelphia’s live weather, with cloud cover and wind from Open-Meteo refreshed every 15 minutes. The city’s Air Management Services readings set the haze. Find both in Sun & sky; weather and air quality remain live when you explore a different date or time.

Building footprints and streets © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Street trees: Philadelphia Parks & Recreation Tree Inventory 2025; streetlights: Streets Department Street Poles inventory (203,000 poles with real heights and bulb types); parkland: PPR Properties boundaries; historic districts and neighborhoods: City of Philadelphia via OpenDataPhilly (CC-BY); bike share status: Indego / Bicycle Transit Systems; street closures and the paving season: Streets Department permits via StreetSmartPHL; historical markers: Pennsylvania Historical and Museum Commission (public domain); public art: Percent for Art, City of Philadelphia via OpenDataPhilly; named places: the City of Philadelphia basemap Landmarks (CC-BY). Traffic volumes: PennDOT Roadway Management System annual average daily traffic (RMSTRAFFIC, open data); the cars are synthesized typical flows at those volumes, not live vehicle positions. Vehicle positions: SEPTA real-time API. Live flights: adsb.fi community ADS-B data. Live ships: AIS via aisstream.io. Live train positions: Amtraker (ODC-By). PATCO trains: PATCO's published timetable, scheduled, not live. Weather: Open-Meteo. Air quality: Philadelphia Air Management Services hourly PM2.5 and ozone. Concert listings: Ticketmaster Discovery (unaffiliated). Scores over the venues on Philadelphia game days and the game days that colour the skyline: ESPN's public scoreboards (unaffiliated). The night's colour on other nights: BOMA Philadelphia's Building Illumination Calendar (unaffiliated). Wall colours in the outer districts: Mapillary street-level imagery, CC BY-SA 4.0. Address search: Nominatim / OpenStreetMap. This is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by SEPTA or Indego. Towers modeled procedurally from photographs and published dimensions.
