Prelink Timing

Round 229 (Stage 1 of the Nov 10 plan): the measurement that decides Stage 3. On this engine, are N programs issued together and then awaited faster than the same N linked one at a time, and what is left at the first draw? The page below is the built city (?dev=1&probe=0); once its button lights, its own __dbg.PERF.linkProbe runs on its own context: copies of the page's heaviest programs (the facade, the ground, the cloud deck, the curtain-wall glass), every copy's fragment source salted with a random constant so no cache has seen it, each drawn once into a small target of the frame's kind with its mesh's vertex layout, then once more. Nothing is kept: every copy is deleted.

Run it: build the page, serve the folder that holds it (python3 -m http.server 8917 from a copy of 3d-model outside Dropbox, with art/ and tests/ beside the page) and open /tests/prelink_gpu.html in Chrome, in Safari and in the iOS simulator's Safari (http://localhost:8917/tests/prelink_gpu.html). Options: ?n=8 programs a run (2 to 48), ?pick=facade,ground,clouds,glass-sys (a name is one of those three families or any part of a program's key), ?ext=1 also asks for KHR_parallel_shader_compile and repeats the runs with it (and once more waiting by its completion poll while the page runs on), ?page=../society-hill-towers.html, ?pq=tier%3D1 passes a query on to the page. Each run compiles programs no cache has seen, so a second visit is as cold as the first.
