Vehicle Lamp Checks

The realism pass: the cars' head and tail lamps (CARLAMP) and the lamps that shine every way (vLampPoints: the aircraft's navigation lights, the ships' lights, the traffic signals' lenses), the production shaders cut from app.js, compiled with the page's log depth and drawn on a real GPU. A lamp far enough off to fall under the sprite's floor must still leave its light (at the floor's two or three pixels every pixel centre sat out on the falloff, and no aircraft ever showed its navigation lights); a head lamp shows from in front and not from behind, a tail by night and on the brake by day; a signal's lens a mile off by day leaves nothing.
