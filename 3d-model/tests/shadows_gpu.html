Shadow Checks

The realism pass: the far shadow map (FARSH's rewrite of r149's light loop, cut from app.js) on a real GPU. A ground receives two boxes' shadows, one inside the near box and one beyond it, from the sun and a black second light: the near shadow from the near map, the far one from the far map while its switch (shadow.radius) is on and none while it is off, and the black light adds no light. Then a tree card (TREE_ART_VERT and treeArtPatch, cut from app.js) is drawn into a sun's map, and the map must hold it at the crown.
